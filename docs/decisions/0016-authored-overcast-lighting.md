# ADR 0016 - Script-authored overcast lighting

Status: accepted (2026-09-26, Direction C approval)

## Context

Direction C, overcast championship morning, is the canonical rowing replay
language. The approved dark companion is blue-hour overcast, not a reduced
exposure of the daylight scene. Runtime procedural-sky processing is tracked
in #67. ADR 0004 disallows even locally generated HDR files, preventing an
offline solution to that work.

## Decision

Supersede only that restriction in ADR 0004: skies generated by reviewed,
seeded scripts in this repository may be committed. Downloaded, imported and
scanned HDRIs remain prohibited. Blender renders metre-scale assets and linear
equirectangular HDR radiance; Qt 6.11.2 balsam prefilters it into KTX1 cubemaps.
The runtime uses the same probe for sky and lighting. Keep all roughness levels
when reducing face resolution, including Qt's final diffuse level.

Start with rowing. Skiing and cycling retain their existing procedural skies.
The scripts, not a hand-edited blend file, are the source of truth. Record
generated outputs in ASSET_PROVENANCE.md and enforce the plain-Git budget from
ADR 0011. Ordinary application builds consume committed outputs and do not
require Blender or run the sky baker.

Use independently authored light and blue-hour sky and fill palettes. Water
keeps its underlying albedo and takes its later-ambient appearance from the
illumination. Keep exposure unchanged between schemes. Clearcoat is expressed through Qt
PrincipledMaterial, not an unsupported importer extension. Low and Medium must
read as diffuse daylight without depending on dynamic shadows.

Retain the circular replay, camera, athlete and equipment contracts. Add fixed
world-space course references, with instancing, and world-fixed water detail.
Do not compensate for the compressed course-distance mapping by speeding up
water texture motion. Any later change to that mapping requires its own decision
and replay-data validation.

## Consequences

This is an intentional visual divergence from the web palette, not a change
to its replay mechanics. The 7.8 m shell and oarlock pivots remain intact.
The existing athlete is exempt from the proposed replacement-rower budget;
the replacement-rower phase was dropped by the owner.

Qt is the visual acceptance renderer. Every comparison must record the runtime
frame and use the same renderer, scheme, tier and replay moment on both sides.
Blender frames are references, not substitutes for Qt validation. A Linux
capture cannot establish macOS/Metal appearance or performance.
