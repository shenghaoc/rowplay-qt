# Asset provenance

Every visual asset rowplay-qt ships is either authored inside the rowplay
repositories or a CC0 material map with recorded provenance. No downloaded
human model, third-party character, scan, likeness, avatar-generator output or
user image contributes to any shipped asset (policy inherited from rowplay's
`static/replay-assets/README.md` and rowplay-studio's `ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`).

Runtime 3D assets are glTF 2.0 `.glb` only (ADR 0003). Image-based lighting is
procedural (ADRs 0004 and 0016): downloaded, imported or scanned HDRIs remain
prohibited; reviewed repository scripts may generate HDR radiance and baked
runtime probes.

## Licences at a glance

| What | Licence |
| --- | --- |
| Application source: Rust, QML, build scripts and `tools/`, including the Blender generation, export and validation tooling | GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`) |
| Common RowPlay-family assets authored here: everything under `assets/replay/authored/` | MIT (`LICENSES/MIT-rowplay.txt`) |
| Assets vendored from rowplay: the V3 rig pack, the V4 athlete's RowPlay work, the baked venues, the icon | MIT (rowplay) |
| Third-party inputs: the V4 athlete's Human Base Meshes base mesh, the Poly Haven material maps | CC0-1.0 (`LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt`) |

ADR 0002 sets the rule. A visual asset meant for reuse across rowplay,
rowplay-studio and rowplay-qt is MIT when two things hold: the owner holds its
copyright, and its provenance is clean. Clean means every input it takes from
elsewhere is recorded, with a licence that allows MIT. How the asset was made
is not the reason. The licence of the tool that made it decides nothing: a
GPL script makes its output neither GPL nor MIT. Inputs keep their own terms.
Every asset has a row below naming its licence and its source, and the
authored pack's inputs are listed with their own licences.

## Common authored assets (MIT)

`assets/replay/authored/` is original work whose copyright the owner,
shenghaoc, holds. Its provenance is clean. No external image, logo, branded
equipment design or HDRI is an input. The inputs it does take from elsewhere
are listed under "Inputs from elsewhere" below: rowplay's MIT work, and a CC0
base mesh that the shell is measured against. So these files are **MIT**

The files are generated by `tools/blender/build_all.py`, `common.py`,
`canonical.py`, `probes.py` and `shell.py`. Those scripts are GPL-3.0-or-later
application tooling. Their licence is not the assets' licence, and it is not
the reason the assets are MIT.

`MANIFEST.json` records the complete output inventory, byte sizes,
SHA-256 pins, Blender version and seed. The asset tests verify those pins and
reject unlisted files. Regenerate with `make blender-assets` (see the pipeline
README). Generated binaries remain in plain Git under ADR 0011. All of them are
procedural assets under ADR 0016's source rule: the scripts are their source,
and no `.blend` file is committed for them.

- `overcast.hdr`, `blue-hour.hdr`: linear equirectangular radiance from Blender
  Cycles world nodes, independently authored daytime and blue-hour palettes.
- `overcast.ktx`, `blue-hour.ktx`: Qt 6.11.2 balsam-prefiltered radiance, reduced
  by the script to 128-pixel faces without dropping any roughness level.
- `water-normal.png`: seeded periodic analytic wave derivatives, no photo input.
- `buoy.glb`: original 224-triangle generic buoy, metre units, plain glTF 2.0.
- `course.json`: 256 fixed world-space placements around the existing loop,
  in two rings of 128 just outside the web's two lanes.
- `rowing-shell.glb` (Blender Phase 2): the single scull and its sculls,
  generated by `tools/blender/shell.py` (with `build_all.py`, `common.py` and
  `canonical.py`) in Blender 5.2.2 LTS on macOS (`make blender-shell`). It is
  procedural: the script is its source, and no `.blend` is committed. Its
  design is generic and original: no brand shapes, logos or reference images
  are inputs. Its contract numbers and its fit come from the inputs below.
  `MANIFEST.json`'s `shell` section records the Blender version and the
  triangle counts.

The athlete and the existing venue packs below are unchanged. The V3 rig
pack is unchanged too and stays vendored and pinned, but the scene now draws
`rowing-shell.glb` in place of its rowing templates and blade leaf. The
upstream licences and provenance of these packs continue to apply: MIT for
rowplay's work, CC0-1.0 for the third-party inputs recorded in their tables.
